Avishai Karawan - Sound Designer & Technical Sound Designer

Hi, I'm Avishai Karawan

I specialize in creating custom tools and workflows that make designing and implementing audio easy, intuitive, and efficient.

Right now I'm the Sound Designer on Tattered Banners, where I design, implement and mix all of the game's audio. I also work as a Sound Designer and Technical Sound Designer at Candivore, creating sounds and maintaining audio pipelines across their live mobile games.

Before that, I was a Sound Designer on Dwarven Realms and a Technical Sound Designer on HUNTERS: Uprising, building tools and using them to implement audio. You can see some of them here. I also spent four years as a Sound Designer at Artlist, recording and designing SFX for the catalog, which you can hear here.

I'm always looking for new ideas and ways to grow, and I've loved sharing what I know as a lecturer at Just Music College.

Thank you for your interest in my work!
